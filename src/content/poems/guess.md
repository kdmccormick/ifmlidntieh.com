Thinking slips I spill it onto the time
Birds keep talking in bird talk
How many forms of life before I give out?
Trace the vasculature of our discontents—how long?
Try not to stand them in for something else
Technology spells death in technicolor

I'm losing my grip and unable to refer
Not even sure of what I would do with referring
Throwing words at the screen
Abating at the abattoir
The end of all conceits
I guess

Writing poetry as a means of avoiding the ground
Good luck grounding poetry in the air
Free floating mechanics loose of any contact
Churning through the bloodways
How to jump and touch sky
Way the hunger creeps in
Cutting loose or tying into the universal network of aches, pains, and sunny afternoons with nothing to do

Replacing weed with alcohol: downgrade
Replacing alcohol with religion: category mistake
Replacing the great chasm of loneliness with "somebody please hold me:" equilibrium out loud

My lithium level taken by a waving needle
Vasculature and discontents I wasn't kidding
Enough stability to make you cut your dreams off
Together on the ground again
Leaving ground brings you down harder than would've been

What now? Give up on an airborne existence?
Make a decent living and then what?
Which version of this is it?
I can barely decide what to have for dinner and I am throwing word salad at the only life I will ever have
Wave the needle and ride the anguish over air?
Still wind with bloodflow and concrete?

I guess
