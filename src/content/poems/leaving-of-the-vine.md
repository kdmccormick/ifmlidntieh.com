---
title: Leaving of the Vine
order: 3
---
Words lodge in the corners of my skull
Cool evening asks if we're there yet
I'm tilling my dreams again
Fallow for so long
Something provisional seeps in the groundwater
Night air toward quiet
Ambiguity in the realness of when
Night earns a lifetime appointment
Droning on forever in as many years

Yet the day is but a single eye and
I dreamed I was rolling one off into the sunset
Never to be seen again
You old cowboy,
Tumbleweed doesn't smoke to keep you warm
And warmth lingering smokes you
As the tumbleweed rolls over your night
I forget to drink water these days
Desert forgets what forest remembers
Only one meets the night with silence
The other sings over eternal harmony
When will you realize?

You are watched all over by the stars
On this clear and sleepless night

You are watched all over by the stars
On this clear and sleepless night

*Abe Brownell, 2026*
